# Image sources

The current enclosure images, `ds-lite-pcb.webp` and `ds-lite-pcb-portrait.webp`, combine color-graded details from Evan-Amos's public-domain [Nintendo DS Lite board photographs (top)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nintendo-DS-Lite-Motherboard-Top.jpg) and [bottom](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nintendo-DS-Lite-Motherboard-Bottom.jpg).

Earlier enclosure images (`pcb-aligned.webp`, `pcb-aligned-portrait.webp`, and `pcb-overhead.webp`) were cropped and color-graded from [Raspberry Pi 4 Model B - Top.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Raspberry_Pi_4_Model_B_-_Top.jpg) by Laserlicht / Wikimedia Commons under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The earlier enclosure used crops of [Computer Motherboard Closeup](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Computer_Motherboard_Closeup.jpg) by Lenharth Systems, released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
